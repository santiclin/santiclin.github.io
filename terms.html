Using vozduh

vozduh is an independently operated PeakCut instance for creating short video clips from longer videos and streams. The TikTok integration lets you connect an account and send clips to TikTok using the permissions you grant.

Your content

Only process and publish content you own or have permission to use. Review generated clips, captions and publishing settings before posting. Follow TikTok’s terms, community guidelines and applicable law.

Connected accounts

You control the account you connect and the publishing settings you choose. You may revoke access in your TikTok account settings. Manage posts already published through TikTok.

Availability

Features may change or become unavailable. Review the Privacy Policy for information about how this instance handles data.
