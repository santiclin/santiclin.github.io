vozduh · Self-hosted PeakCut

Privacy Policy

Updated November 24, 2026

Scope

This policy covers vozduh, a self-hosted PeakCut instance operated by santiclin, and its optional TikTok integration.

Information used

The app processes video sources and generated clips for editing and export. If you connect TikTok, the app uses account identifiers, any basic profile information returned by TikTok, and authorization tokens to manage the connection and carry out publishing actions you configure.

Service providers

TikTok receives videos and publishing details when the integration sends clips to your account. If an external AI provider is configured, the app can send material such as transcript excerpts to that provider for clip selection. GitHub Pages hosts this policy.

Storage and removal

The PeakCut instance stores media and connection data as needed for its features. You can revoke TikTok access in your TikTok settings. To request removal of data held by this instance, contact its operator. Use TikTok to manage clips already published on your account.
